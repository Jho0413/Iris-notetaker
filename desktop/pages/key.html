Connect Iris to OpenAI

Iris records and processes everything on this Mac, then sends the audio and the moments that matter on screen to OpenAI to write the notes. Calls are billed to your OpenAI account, about $0.50–2 per hour of session.

Create a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys. It is stored encrypted with your macOS Keychain, and only Iris's engine on this Mac uses it.
